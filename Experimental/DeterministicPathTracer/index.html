Per-pixel gives each pixel its own fixed scramble of the same point set (no RNG). Rasterized finds camera hits with a raster pass. Reproject keeps the image while the camera moves and fades it out as exact samples arrive (clamped to the current neighbourhood). The denoiser smooths 1–4 spp along normal, depth and material edges and fades out by 64 spp, so the converged image stays exact. Vehicles parks extra copies behind the hero car (Fleet camera shows all).

Method

  • One fixed Sobol' (0,2) point set, shared by every pixel by default — no RNG at any setting.
  • Every lobe traced: coat, specular and diffuse; glass splits into reflection and refraction.
  • No Russian roulette: fixed depth limits and a fixed throughput cutoff.
  • Softboxes integrated by light quadrature + BSDF quadrature, power-heuristic MIS.
  • Error is structured and shrinks every power of two; it never sparkles.
